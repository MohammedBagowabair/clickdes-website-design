The studio

Designing with heart

ClickDes was founded in Mukalla with a simple belief: a beautiful space can change the way you feel every single day.

01 — Founder & creative director

Omar
Ba'alawi

Trained in interior architecture and shaped by a decade of residential and hospitality work, Omar leads every ClickDes project personally.

His approach blends the warmth of Hadramout's heritage — clay, arches, courtyards — with the clarity of contemporary design.

“A home should hold you like the late afternoon light.”

Philosophy

What guides every space we shape

  • 01

    Colour as architecture

    We use colour to shape space — to warm, to calm, to guide the eye through a room.

  • 02

    Rooted in place

    Clay, stone and arches of Hadramout inform a contemporary, timeless language.

  • 03

    Crafted by hand

    Custom joinery, local artisans and honest materials that age beautifully.

  • 04

    Light first

    Every layout begins with how daylight moves through the home across the day.

Design process

Six steps, one vision

A clear, transparent process — so you always know what happens next.

  1. 01

    Discovery

    A first conversation and site visit to understand how you live, work and dream.

  2. 02

    Concept

    Mood, palette and spatial ideas presented as a clear, inspiring direction.

  3. 03

    Design development

    Detailed layouts, 3D visualisation, materials and bespoke furniture.

  4. 04

    Technical drawings

    Precise documentation so every contractor builds exactly what was designed.

  5. 05

    Execution

    On-site supervision, procurement and coordination with trusted craftsmen.

  6. 06

    Handover & styling

    Final styling and a calm handover of a space that is ready to live in.
